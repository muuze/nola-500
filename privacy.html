Privacy Policy

Nola's 500, by Muuze Games. Effective November 9, 2026.

Nola's 500 does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking in the app, and Muuze Games runs no servers for it.

What stays on your device

The name you type and any saved game are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

What other players see

During a nearby or online game, your name and your moves are sent to the other players in that game so you can play together. Nearby games connect devices directly using Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. This information isn't sent to Muuze Games.

Apple services

Online play uses Apple's Game Center, and the multiplayer purchase uses Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy. Muuze Games receives only Apple's standard sales reports, which don't identify you.

Permissions

The app asks for local network and Bluetooth access only to find and connect to nearby players.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
