Common questions

Do I have to pay to play?

No. Playing with computer players is free. Nearby and online multiplayer is a one-time $0.99 in-app purchase, and each player who wants to play multiplayer needs the unlock.

I bought multiplayer, but it's locked.

Tap any locked option on the main menu, then tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

I can't find a nearby game.

Turn on Wi-Fi on both devices, and keep the app open on both. You don't need to join a network. When the app asks to find devices on your local network, tap Allow. You can change this in Settings, under Nola's 500. You don't need an internet connection. Connecting can take several seconds.

I can't play online.

Online play uses Game Center. Check that you're signed in under Settings, then Game Center, and that both players have an internet connection.

What happens if someone leaves?

The game pauses, and the host can wait for them to rejoin or have a computer take their seat. If the person hosting leaves, the game ends for everyone.
